Gatefall Privacy Policy

Effective: [date of first release]

Short version: Gatefall has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases. Your decks and settings stay on your device. Only when you play online with friends does the game send the player name you typed and your game moves to our room server, which passes them to the other players in your room and forgets them soon after.

Who we are

Gatefall is made by [your name or studio name]. Questions about this policy: [contact email address].

Data that stays on your device

The game stores these things on your device only, in the app's own storage (or your browser's storage when you play on the web):

  • your decks, captured boss cards and unlocked Duelists;
  • the player name you choose;
  • settings such as sound, music, phase banners and reaction time.

We never receive this data. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app (on the web: clearing the site's data in your browser). The Save code feature only shows this data (and a solo duel in progress) as text on your screen so you can copy it yourself; it is not uploaded anywhere.

Online multiplayer

Solo duels, boss hunts, free-for-all and 2v2 against the computer work without sending anything. When you host or join an online room, the game sends the following to our room server:

  • the player name you typed (we recommend a nickname, not your real name);
  • a random player ID the game creates for that session (it is not linked to you or your device and changes when you close the game);
  • the room code and the moves of the duel (cards played, attacks, choices).

The server passes these to the other players in the same room, so they can see your name and play against you. That is the only purpose. The server keeps this information only in its working memory: it is not written to a database or log. A player is removed about 90 seconds after they stop connecting, and a room with nobody in it is deleted after at most 6 hours. Restarting the server deletes everything.

Like every internet service, the server receives your device's IP address while it talks to you. The game's server software does not store it. Our hosting provider, [hosting provider], may keep short technical logs under its own privacy policy.

All connections to the room server are encrypted (HTTPS).

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and no third-party code that collects data. The game's fonts and art are part of the game, so it loads nothing from other websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files are accessed.

The store you download the game from (for example Google Play, the App Store or Steam) handles downloads, updates and any reviews you write, under that store's own privacy policy.

Children

Gatefall is not directed at children under 13. Because the game has no accounts and keeps no personal data on our side, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has used a real name in an online room, contact us; room data is deleted automatically in any case.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California) you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Since online room data is deleted automatically within hours and everything else stays on your device, there is normally nothing stored by us to hand over or delete, but you are always welcome to ask at the address above. The legal basis for relaying your name and moves is the performance of the online game you asked to play.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date, and in the store listing.
